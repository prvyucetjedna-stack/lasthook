This policy explains what data the LAST HOOK: 30 ROOFTOPS game ("the App") handles, why, and what
choices you have. It is based on what the App's code actually does.

1. Summary

  • The App has no user accounts, no registration, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting. The developer runs no server and does not receive your personal data.
  • An internet connection is required: the App does not start without a working (validated) internet connection, because the game is funded by ads that must be loaded online.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain data (e.g. IP address, advertising ID, device information), as described in section 4.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown where required, and you can change your choice later in the App.

3. Data stored on your device only

The App stores the following in its private app storage (Android SharedPreferences):

  • game progress: best section and percentage, number of attempts, whether you finished the route, whether the tutorial was completed, unlocked and selected robot looks;
  • a counter used for ad pacing (number of deaths since the last ad opportunity);
  • settings: music, sound effects, vibration, reduced effects, chosen language.

This data never leaves your device through the App. If Android Backup is enabled on your device,
Android may include this app data in your device backup (Google's Auto Backup), which is governed by
your Google account settings. The Google UMP SDK also stores your consent choice on the device.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob and Google UMP

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (version 25.5.0) to show full-screen (interstitial) ads at
certain moments after a game over, and the Google User Messaging Platform SDK (version 4.0.0) to
obtain consent where required.

According to Google's official disclosure, the Google Mobile Ads SDK **automatically collects and
shares** the following for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • IP address – which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • User product interactions – e.g. app launches, taps, video views;
  • Diagnostic information – e.g. app launch time, hang rate, energy usage;
  • Device and account identifiers – the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

Google states that this data is encrypted in transit (TLS). This data is collected and processed by
Google, not by the developer. Depending on your consent and settings, ads may be personalised or
non-personalised.

Official sources:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK – Google Play data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google User Messaging Platform: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy
  • Google ad settings / My Ad Center: https://myadcenter.google.com/

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland): where required, a Google consent message is shown at launch. When required, a "Privacy options" button appears in the App's Settings so you can change or withdraw your choice at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Ad personalisation: you can control it at https://myadcenter.google.com/.

Legal basis (GDPR): where the consent message requires it, ads-related processing for storing or
accessing information on your device and for personalised ads is based on your consent

described in Google's message (e.g. non-personalised ads, fraud prevention, measurement) may rely on
legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) as stated in that message.

5. Gameplay videos, saving and sharing

  • When you tap "Share run" or "Save video", the App creates a short MP4 video (or, if video encoding is unavailable, a PNG image) on your device from the game state of your last attempt. It contains only game graphics and game sound – no camera, microphone or screen recording.
  • The file is first written to the App's private cache (exports/); it is replaced at the next export and removed when the app's cache is cleared or the App is uninstalled.
  • Save video copies the file to your gallery folder Movies/LastHook. It stays there until you delete it (also after uninstalling the App).
  • Share on TikTok (only when you tap it) passes the file directly to the TikTok app installed on your device, with a temporary read permission; TikTok then opens its own editor and you decide whether to post. The App contains no TikTok SDK, uses no TikTok account or key and sends nothing to TikTok by itself. To offer this button, the App checks locally whether a TikTok app is installed; this check is not transmitted anywhere. If TikTok is not installed, you can pick another app or open TikTok's Google Play page. Once shared, TikTok's privacy policy applies: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/eea/privacy-policy/en
  • Share run opens the Android share sheet. The file is passed only to the app you choose, with a temporary read permission. What that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy.

  • The developer holds no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side; you may still contact us with any request.
